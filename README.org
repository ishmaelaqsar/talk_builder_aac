* Talk Builder AAC - Sentence Building for Early Intervention

Talk Builder AAC is a Flutter application designed for children (ages 2-5) who are ready to transition from single words to building sentences.

Unlike general AAC apps that can be overwhelming, this app bridges the gap between simple "choice boards" and complex communication systems. It introduces the concept of a **Sentence Strip** while maintaining the **safety** and **simplicity** of the original /Toddler Talk/ philosophy.

** 🌟 Features

*** 🗣️ Builder Mode (The Default)
- **Visual Sentence Strip:** Tapping icons adds them to a top bar, allowing the child to construct thoughts (for example, "I" + "Want" + "Apple") before speaking.
- **"Speak" Button:** A large, distinct button reads the whole sentence at a natural pace, and highlights each word as it is spoken. The strip scrolls to keep that word in view. While a sentence plays, the same button stops it.
- **New Sentence After Speak:** When a sentence plays to its end, the next card starts a new sentence. The child does not have to clear the old one first.
- **Correction Tools:** A "Backspace" and a "Clear" button. "Clear" offers "Undo". You can also tap a word in the strip to hear it again, or hold it to take it out.
- **Pinned Core Words:** All eight core words stay on screen above your own cards, so they never scroll out of reach. On a narrow screen they sit in two rows of four.
- **British Voice:** The device voice speaks British English by default. You can choose an American voice, and a slower speed, in **Settings**.
- **Stays Put:** Back does nothing, and the system bars stay hidden, so a stray swipe does not take the child out of the app. The screen stays on while the app is open.
- **Sage Green Theme:** A calming, focus-oriented colour palette that sets this "big kid" app apart from the toddler version.

*** 👩‍👦 Mom Mode (Admin)
- **Safety Lock:** **Press and hold** the lock icon (top right) for 1.5 seconds. A ring fills while you hold. Then a gate names three digits in words. Tap them in that order.
- **Locks Itself:** Tap the lock to leave admin mode. Admin mode also locks after two minutes without a touch, and when the app goes to the background. It does not lock while you edit a card, change the settings, or back up or import.
- **Clear Marks:** The title says "Admin mode", and a red border marks each card that you can edit. A hidden card shows dimmed.
- **Card Editor:** Tap one of your cards to open it. Change its picture or its word, hide it from your child, or delete it.
- **Drag to Move:** Hold a card and drag it to a new place. The app saves the new order at once.
- **Custom Photos:** Take a photo, or choose one you already have, to add your child's own toys, food, or people.
- **Symbol Library:** 452 built-in symbols, searchable by name. When you add a card from a symbol, the symbol's name fills in the word.
- **Hide, Don't Delete:** Switch off **Show to your child** to take a card off your child's grid and keep it.
- **Guarded Deletes:** A delete asks first, and says that hiding is the reversible option.
- **Settings:** A British or an American voice, a normal or a slow speed, and small, medium, or large cards. Each change applies at once.
- **Backup and Import:** Save your cards and their photos to a dated zip file. Add the cards of a Talk Builder or a *Toddler Talk* backup.
- **Safe Load Errors:** If the app cannot read the saved cards, it says so and offers **Try again**. The core words keep working, and nothing overwrites the saved cards.

** 📖 Parent Guide

*** How to Edit Cards
1. Open the app.
2. Find the **Lock Icon** in the top right corner.
3. **Press and hold** it for about 1.5 seconds. A ring fills as you hold. Release when the gate opens. With a screen reader, use the lock's long-press action instead.
4. The **Grown-ups only** gate names three digits in words, for example "Tap seven, two, five". Tap those keys in that order. A wrong key, **Cancel**, a tap outside the gate, or 10 seconds with no key closes it. Hold the lock again to retry.
5. You now see:
   - A "+" button (**Add a card**). Take a photo, choose one from the gallery, or pick one from the **Symbol library**. Then type what the card says, up to 24 characters, and tap **Save**.
   - A gear button (**Settings and backup**) for **Settings**, **Back up cards**, and **Import or restore cards**.
   - A red border on each of your cards. Tap a card to open **Card details**. There you can change its picture or its word, switch off **Show to your child** to hide it, or tap **Delete card**.
   - Hold a card and drag it to move it.
6. Tap the lock when you finish. Admin mode also locks after two minutes without a touch.

*** How to change the voice and the card size
1. Enter admin mode.
2. Tap the gear button (top right), then **Settings**.
3. Choose the **Voice** (British or American), the **Speed** (Normal or Slow), and the **Card size** (Small, Medium, or Large). The app applies and saves each change at once. **Hear it** speaks "I want more." with the voice and the speed that you chose.

The card size applies to your own cards. The core words keep their own layout.

*** How to back up and restore
1. Enter admin mode.
2. Tap the gear button (top right).
3. Choose:
   - **Back up cards:** Makes a zip file of your cards and their photos, named ~talk_builder_backup_YYYY-MM-DD.zip~, and opens the share sheet. Save it to Google Drive, or send it to yourself.
   - **Import or restore cards:** Select a Talk Builder or a *Toddler Talk* backup. Its cards join the cards that you have. The import skips a card that is already here, so a second import of the same file adds nothing.

A backup from one device restores on another. The settings are not in the backup.

*** How to Import from "Toddler Talk"
1. In *Toddler Talk*, enter parent mode: press and hold the lock, then tap the three digits that the gate names.
2. Tap the three-dot menu (top right), then **Backup Data**. Save the ~.zip~ file where this device can reach it, for example in Google Drive.
3. Open *Talk Builder*, and enter admin mode.
4. Tap the gear button (top right), then **Import or restore cards**.
5. Select the ~.zip~ file.

Photo, GIF, symbol, and emoji cards arrive with their words, their colours, and whether they are hidden. Recordings, pages, settings, and the speech text of a card (**What it says (optional)** in *Toddler Talk*) do not arrive. Talk Builder speaks the word of each card with the device voice, and a message says how many recordings it left out.

Talk Builder reads both *Toddler Talk* backup formats: the older one with ~data.json~, and the newer one with ~cards.json~.

*** If the cards do not load
If the app cannot read the saved cards, the grid says "The cards could not be loaded" and offers **Try again**. Try that first. The core words keep working. The **Add a card** button and the gear menu stay hidden, so nothing overwrites the saved cards.

In admin mode, **Start again** also shows. It keeps a copy of the unreadable cards on the device, then starts with no cards of your own. The app cannot show the old cards again. Your child sees only **Try again**, so a child cannot clear the cards.

*** ⚠️ Critical: Setting up "Kiosk Mode"
To prevent your child from closing the app, you must enable **App Pinning** on your Android device. Builder mode ignores Back and hides the system bars, but only App Pinning stops the Home gesture:
1. Go to *Settings > Security > Advanced > App Pinning*.
2. Turn it *ON*.
3. Open Talk Builder.
4. Swipe up to see recent apps, tap the app icon, and select *Pin*.

With a local Flutter SDK:
#+BEGIN_SRC bash
flutter test
#+END_SRC

Without one, ~tool/verify.sh~ runs the same checks that CI runs, inside a
container:
#+BEGIN_SRC bash
tool/verify.sh          # dart format check, flutter analyze, flutter test
tool/verify.sh format   # rewrite files with dart format
tool/verify.sh shell    # a shell inside the container
#+END_SRC

CI pins its Flutter version in ~.github/workflows/main.yml~. Run the checks on
that version before you push, so a local pass means a CI pass.

When the image carries a different Flutter, clone that Flutter release on the
host and set ~FLUTTER_SDK~ to it. The checkout replaces the image's own SDK.
Keep it in a folder that Docker can mount, for example under your home folder.

#+BEGIN_SRC bash
git clone --branch <version> https://github.com/flutter/flutter.git <dir>
FLUTTER_SDK=<dir> tool/verify.sh
#+END_SRC

CI runs ~dart format --set-exit-if-changed~, so run the format step before you
commit.

** ♿ Accessibility Notes

- Every card and every icon-only control carries a screen-reader label.
- In admin mode, a card tells a screen reader that a tap edits it, and whether
  it is hidden from your child.
- The lock needs a 1.5-second hold, which some people cannot make. A screen
  reader offers the same unlock as the lock's long-press action.
- Touch targets meet the 48dp minimum, including **Add a card**, **Delete card**,
  and the **Show to your child** switch.
- The layout holds at a system text scale of 2.0. Tile text scales with a
  ~FittedBox~ instead of overflowing.
- Card deletes ask first. ~Clear~ offers ~Undo~.
- Admin mode locks itself again after two minutes without a touch.
