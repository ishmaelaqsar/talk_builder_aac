* Talk Builder AAC - Sentence Building for Early Intervention

Talk Builder AAC is a Flutter application designed for children (ages 2-5) who are ready to transition from single words to building sentences.

Unlike general AAC apps that can be overwhelming, this app bridges the gap between simple "choice boards" and complex communication systems. It introduces the concept of a **Sentence Strip** while maintaining the **safety** and **simplicity** of the original /Toddler Talk/ philosophy.

** 🌟 Features

*** 🗣️ Builder Mode (The Default)
- **Visual Sentence Strip:** Tapping icons adds them to a top bar, allowing the child to construct thoughts (for example, "I" + "Want" + "Apple") before speaking.
- **"Speak" Button:** A large, distinct button reads the whole sentence at a natural pace, and highlights each word as it is spoken. While a sentence plays, the same button stops it.
- **Correction Tools:** A "Backspace" and a "Clear" button. "Clear" offers "Undo". You can also tap a word in the strip to hear it again, or hold it to take it out.
- **Pinned Core Words:** The eight core words stay on screen above the photo grid, so they never scroll out of reach.
- **Sage Green Theme:** A calming, focus-oriented colour palette that sets this "big kid" app apart from the toddler version.

*** 👩‍👦 Mom Mode (Admin)
- **Safety Lock:** Access editing tools by **Long Pressing** the lock icon (top right).
- **Custom Photos:** Take a photo, or choose one you already have, to add your child's own toys, food, or people.
- **Visibility Toggles:** "Hide" cards that are no longer relevant, without deleting them.
- **Rename and Reorder:** Hold a card to rename it. Use the arrows to move it.
- **Guarded Deletes:** A delete asks first, and says that hiding is the reversible option.
- **Auto-Relock:** Admin mode locks itself again after two minutes without a tap, so live delete buttons never sit in a child's hands.
- **Backward Compatibility:** Seamlessly import backups from *Toddler Talk* so you don't have to retake all your photos.

** 📖 Parent Guide

*** How to Edit Cards
1. Open the app.
2. Find the **Lock Icon** in the top right corner.
3. **Press and Hold** (Long Press) the lock until you see "Admin mode".
4. You will now see:
   - A "+" button to add new photos.
   - "Delete" and "Hide" icons on custom cards.
   - A "Settings" gear for Backup/Restore.

*** How to Import from "Toddler Talk"
1. In your old *Toddler Talk* app, go to Settings -> **Backup Data** and save the ~.zip~ file.
2. Open *Talk Builder*.
3. Enter **Admin Mode** (Long Press Lock).
4. Tap the **Settings Gear** and select **Import from Toddler Talk**.
5. Select the ~.zip~ file you created. Your photos will appear automatically!

*** ⚠️ Critical: Setting up "Kiosk Mode"
To prevent your child from closing the app, you must enable **App Pinning** on your Android device:
1. Go to *Settings > Security > Advanced > App Pinning*.
2. Turn it *ON*.
3. Open Talk Builder.
4. Swipe up to see recent apps, tap the app icon, and select *Pin*.

** 🧪 Testing

The test suite covers the sentence strip, the safety locks on destructive
actions, the accessibility labels, and layout at four viewports and three text
scales.

With a local Flutter SDK:
#+BEGIN_SRC bash
flutter test
#+END_SRC

Without one, ~tool/verify.sh~ runs the same checks that CI runs, inside a
container:
#+BEGIN_SRC bash
tool/verify.sh          # dart format check, flutter analyze, flutter test
tool/verify.sh format   # rewrite files with dart format
tool/verify.sh shell    # a shell inside the container
#+END_SRC

CI runs ~dart format --set-exit-if-changed~, so run the format step before you
commit.

** ♿ Accessibility Notes

- Every card and every icon-only control carries a screen-reader label.
- Touch targets meet the 48dp minimum, including the admin delete and hide
  buttons.
- The layout holds at a system text scale of 2.0. Tile text scales with a
  ~FittedBox~ instead of overflowing.
- Card deletes ask first. ~Clear~ offers ~Undo~.
- Admin mode locks itself again after two minutes without a tap.
